What I do

AI products in the enterprise
I work with technical buyers to find out whether an AI product fits their data, their stack and their appetite for risk. Then I build the demo or proof of concept that settles the question.
Data and analytics integration
APIs, SDKs, the HTTP layer and data warehouse integrations. I’m comfortable in the implementation detail, which is usually where an evaluation is won or lost.
Data governance across APAC
Residency and compliance rules differ a lot between Singapore, India, China and Japan. I help teams design around them early, before they surface in procurement.
Teaching sales teams
I run AI enablement for account teams, and build tools that help other solutions engineers move faster.
