About me

I started out writing software, then found I liked the conversations around it more: what a team is trying to do, what’s in the way, and whether the thing in front of them will actually help. That’s been my job ever since.

I still build things to stay honest. This year that has meant a retrieval pipeline and a personal agent, mostly to understand what enterprise teams run into when they put AI into production.

Away from work I’m usually underwater or at a table. I’m a nitrox-certified diver, and I eat my way around Singapore between trips.
